FAQ

Common Questions

How quickly will a technician arrive?
Our technicians typically arrive within 2–4 hours of booking. For urgent cases, we try our best to dispatch same-day.
What brands do you service?
We service all major brands including KENT, Aquaguard, Pureit, Livpure, AO Smith, HUL, Blue Star, Eureka Forbes and more.
Do you provide a warranty on service?
Yes! All our services come with a 30-day warranty. If the same issue recurs within 30 days, we'll fix it free of charge.
What is AMC and why do I need it?
AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) includes 2 scheduled visits, filter checks, and priority service for the whole year. It's ideal if you want hassle-free pure water year-round.
Are there any hidden charges?
No! We believe in 100% transparent pricing. The technician will provide a full quote before starting any work. You pay only what was agreed.
